# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first. These double as the store release notes.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- Faster and lighter: Lenderoo no longer keeps checking your favourites in
  the background, which was using battery and data while the app sat open.
- Friends list, Library's "+" and other buttons look right again, and
  buttons respond when you press them.
- Readable status bar over item photos and on the sign-in screen.
- Clearer toasts that disappear on their own.
- Item cards line up: one-line and two-line names make the same size card,
  and buttons stay on one line.
- Sideways rows fade at the edge to show there's more to scroll.
- Tidier borrow request cards; avatars show properly on iPhone.
- Light and dark mode no longer get mixed up.
- Send Feedback has moved to Settings → Support.
- A small ad bar on some browsing screens helps cover running costs (not on
  Settings or forms, and not in your first week). _Off until switched on._
- Approving a request for something that's lent out says it puts them next in
  the queue.
- See someone's history with your things (what they borrowed, and whether it
  came back on time) before you approve their request.
- Take someone out of an item's queue.
- Invite a friend: share your friend code and a link to the app from Add
  Friend.
- The barcode scanner shows a frame to line the barcode up in.
- Made for iPad too: content sits in a readable column, grids use the extra
  width, request cards and friends go two-up, and the tab bar stays compact.
- Built on Expo SDK 58 for iOS 27.
